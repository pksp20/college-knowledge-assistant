# Test questions and expected results

Run `pytest -q` for automated checks. Use the following questions in the UI with the included sample documents; upload the conflicting handbook as well for scenario 5.

| # | Question | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What attendance is required to take the final exam? | Returns the 75% requirement with attendance policy citation. |
| 2 | When does the final exam period start and how much is the fee? | Returns 12 May 2025 and 1,200 rupees, citing the exam notice. |
| 3 | Who won the latest World Cup? | Declines because the documents do not say. |
| 4 | How much class participation do I need before I can take finals? | Retrieves the 75% attendance rule despite paraphrased wording. |
| 5 | What attendance is needed for finals? (upload both policy and 2024 handbook) | Flags disagreement and displays both sources. |
| 6 | What should applicants include with their application? | Lists the form, marksheet, identity proof, and photographs. |
| 7 | Can I borrow six books at once? | Says the documents do not support that; the library limit is four. |
| 8 | What is the library loan duration and late fee? | Gives fourteen days and 10 rupees per book per day. |
| 9 | Where can I reset my college password? | Gives the IT help desk in the administration building. |
| 10 | What time is the IT help desk open? | Gives Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–4:00 PM. |
